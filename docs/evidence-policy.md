# Evidence Policy

## Relationship status

- `confirmed`: the cited source explicitly supports the relationship type.
- `documented_deployment_relationship`: the source documents deployment, leasing, or use, but does not by itself establish a direct-customer classification.
- No relationship is inferred solely from press coverage, product usage, or common industry knowledge.

## Source priority

1. SEC filings and exhibits
2. NVIDIA official investor/newsroom releases
3. Counterparty primary disclosures
4. Reputable secondary sources only when primary evidence is unavailable

## Reproducibility

Every record stores its publication date, source URL, locator, and research cutoff. Snapshot fixtures contain representative records used for deterministic tests.
